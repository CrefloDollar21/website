Player Style: 3-and-D Slasher

A "3-and-D" player is valued mainly for two things: knocking down three-point shots and playing tough, reliable defense on the perimeter. A "3-and-D slasher" adds another layer to that role — instead of only spotting up for catch-and-shoot threes, this player also attacks closeouts and drives hard to the basket, using quickness to slash past defenders and finish or kick the ball out. On defense, they use length, activity, and effort to disrupt passing lanes and lock up opposing scorers. This combination makes them valuable role players who can space the floor, create their own offense off the dribble, and hold their own defensively against multiple positions.

Overview

Combo guard with strong ball-handling skills and a quick first step. Effective scorer off the dribble and shows good court vision as a secondary playmaker. Needs to improve consistency on catch-and-shoot opportunities and defensive positioning against bigger guards.

Offense

Shooting: Solid mid-range shooter, streaky from three-point range. Shooting mechanics are clean but release can be slow under pressure.

Ball Handling: Excellent handle in isolation, able to change speeds and break down defenders one-on-one.

Passing: Good court vision, finds cutters and shooters in transition. Occasionally forces passes into traffic.

Finishing: Crafty around the rim, uses floaters and euro-steps to avoid contact. Could add strength to finish through contact.

Defense

On-Ball: Active hands, generates steals in passing lanes. Can be beaten by quicker guards off the dribble.

Off-Ball: Solid awareness rotating for help defense, but loses focus closing out on shooters.

Rebounding: Below average for position, does not consistently box out.

Athleticism

Above average quickness and lateral movement. Average vertical explosiveness. Good conditioning, plays hard for full possessions.

Intangibles

Vocal leader on the court, competitive mentality. Coachable and receptive to feedback. Needs to improve decision-making in late-game situations.

Strengths

Shooting, defense, ball handling, shot creation, quickness, competitiveness

Weaknesses

Three-point consistency

Left hand Finishing
